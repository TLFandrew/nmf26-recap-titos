Deliverables

What we delivered for Tito's.

Tito's Lantern Punch led the menu at both NightMarket.fun bars, the general bar and the VIP bar.

  • Tito's Lantern Punch as the signature cocktailServed at both bars, finished with a dried citrus wheel, fresh basil and a light-up ice cube.
  • Top billing on the bar menusFirst item on the hanging menu and the counter menus, with the Tito's logo on the menu board.
  • A non-alcoholic Lantern PunchA Tito's-named zero-proof version on the same menus, for non-drinkers and designated drivers.
  • Tito's prize box in the Passport appA featured raffle prize, marked 21+, that guests entered with points earned around the festival.
  • Tito's on the app's partner wallListed with the festival's Community Supporters in the Passport app.

Built for a night out.

The bar was 21 and over, and we made it easy for guests to leave the car at home, with free CapMetro Red Line passes, a free shuttle from Lakeline Station, and Cedar Park's LiNK rides. Rain arrived around 8:30 pm and we closed early around 9:30, cutting the bar's last hour short.

Charitable impact

What Tito's support made possible.

171

performers, musicians and dancers from 22 acts across three stages

~100

booths: 35 artists and makers, 39 food vendors, and 23 local businesses and community organizations

Tito's support underwrote the festival's signature cocktail program, which kept the cost of the bar off the festival budget and left more of it for the artists, performers and volunteers who make the night.

A signature cocktail program also makes NightMarket.fun a night out for adults, and adult guests are the ones who buy art, book performers and come back to local businesses after the festival. That spending is what makes the festival sustainable for AANHPI artists year-round, not just one night a year.

2027

Here's to next year.

Tito's Lantern Punch gave the 2026 bar a signature, and guests carried it all over the park. In 2027 we'd love to build on it with an earlier start, putting the Lantern Punch in more of the festival's photo moments and giving Tito's its own moment in the Passport app.

We'd love to talk about growing with Tito's at NightMarket.fun 2027.
